# Sion District Portal

## What This Is

A localized web portal for the Sion District in Belo Horizonte, Minas Gerais. It serves as a digital hub to showcase local shops and includes a marketplace system for users to buy and sell items within the community.

## Core Value

Connecting local residents and businesses in Sion District through a unified, easy-to-use local commerce and directory platform.
